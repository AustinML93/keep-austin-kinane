HERE’S HOW WE GET DOWN

As one of the biggest comedy festivals in North America, Moontower brings a TWO-WEEK comedy onslaught to the heart of downtown Austin, TX every April. Say whaaa? That means over 150 of the world’s top and emerging comedians performing everywhere from fancy arena shows to the sweet, sticky floors of intimate comedy clubs during our 4-day comedy binge. Ya just need to grab a badge for the club binge, then add on any single tickets to your must-see headliner shows in the larger theatres!

SEEIN’ IS BELIEVIN’

2 WEEKS OF HEADLINERS

The nation’s top touring comics are headed to Paramount & State Theatres and more. Single tickets needed – badges get presale access!

4-DAY CLUB BINGE

Over 100 comedians invade downtown Austin April 15-18 at 10+ walkable comedy clubs. A badge gets you into ALL of this!

PARTY AT THE MOONTOWER

Top off every night of the Club Series with us, including our big Closing Night Party. Exclusive for badge holders and comics!

And Beyond

Past years have mixed comedy with brisket, bull riding, golf, painting, anonymous audience secrets…anything can and will happen.
